## Decision

kms deprecates `roadmap` (replaced by `decide`), `refactor-plan`, `brainstorm`, `clarify` and `changelog` for one release. go-getter keeps `roadmap` as decomposition into units, keeps `refactor-plan` as a decompose variant and `brainstorm`, folds `clarify` into `discover` as its interview mechanic and `changelog` into `release`.

## Why

Plans, options and release output are coordination work, not durable knowledge.
