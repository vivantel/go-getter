title: The generated CI workflow takes permissions, concurrency and action refs from an active decision's ci-workflow key

`go-getter apply` writes `.github/workflows/go-getter-checks.yml` from the `go-getter.ci-workflow` frontmatter key of the first active decision that carries it (`ciWorkflow()` in `compiler/src/apply.mjs`). All fields are optional; absent, the output is unchanged.

- `permissions: per-job` puts `contents: read` on each job instead of at the top level.
- `concurrency: {group, cancel-in-progress}` adds a top-level `concurrency` block.
- `checkout` and `setup-node` replace the pinned `actions/checkout` and `actions/setup-node` refs (`owner/repo@sha # version`).

The first line, `# Generated by go-getter apply; do not edit.`, is the marker for linters and tests to recognise generated workflows.
