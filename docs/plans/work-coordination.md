### K2 Claim, lease, heartbeat, release — [x]
Class: implement · Effort: high · Needs: K1 · Check: `node --test compiler/test/work-claim.test.mjs`
Context: decision 0166; guardrails `work-only-under-a-live-claim`, `a-claim-names-its-holder`. Git is already the substrate (checkpoints are hidden refs, `compiler/src/checkpoint.mjs`; worktrees, `compiler/src/worktree.mjs`). Work sources are read through `callSource` in `compiler/src/trackers/worksource.mjs`; tracker writes follow the project's answer (decision 0160, guardrail `tracker-writes-follow-the-answer`) and only through the `plan issue` verbs. No new runtime dependency.
Do: add `compiler/src/work/scope.mjs` (overlap of two scopes: globs, owned interfaces, shared artifacts and decision tags declared as shared artifacts, decision 0167 item 1; K3 and `run next` use it), `compiler/src/work/claim.mjs` and `go-getter work claim|heartbeat|release <unit>`, exactly as decision 0166 items 1, 2 and 5 say. Claims are entries in one ledger per feature, the remote ref `refs/go-getter/claims/<feature-stem>`, one file per entry. An entry records unit, holder (kind and id), issuer, scope tokens, `created`, lease expiry, brief version (a hash of the brief fields only), state with counters, grant, parent entry and, for a delegated unit that has not merged, its brief fields capped by `brief-max` (default 8 KB; larger spills to the tracker issue); the merging PR writes the unit into the plan file and drops the entry's copy. A unit with live children holds no scope tokens. A claim fetches the ledger, checks in that version that no live entry holds the same unit or an overlapping scope, adds its entry in a commit it authors and pushes it as a fast-forward of the version it read; a rejected push re-fetches, re-checks and retries with bounded backoff; force-push is never used. Renewal is a fast-forward of `refs/go-getter/heartbeat/<worker-id>` (a tiny commit), made by `heartbeat`, by every `go-getter` verb and by the generated pre-commit and pre-push hooks, only when less than half the lease remains and never offline, together with the unit's `refs/go-getter/checkpoints/<unit>`; ledger, heartbeat and checkpoint pushes use a re-entry guard so the hooks do not fire on them. A lease lapses at its expiry or its holder's last heartbeat plus the lease, plus `clock-skew` (default 5 min); a worker compares its clock with the host's `Date` header on every fetch and refuses to claim when they differ by more than the margin. Leases are pack answers: `lease-agent` 60 min, `lease-ci` the job's limit, `lease-human` 8 h. A unit with live children has no lease, except that an idle parent (no action by its holder on it or on a child for `parent-idle`, default 7 days) is flagged `idle` (K7 parks it with an ask); a parked unit holds a non-expiring `parked` state. An entry is valid only when authored by its holder or issuer, and its grant, issuer and scope are immutable: readers ignore a violating entry. Every write runs the secret and personal-data scan (`compiler/src/secrets/patterns.mjs`) and refuses a match. A state change (claimed, released, expired) posts one comment on the unit's issue when tracker writes allow it; renewal never touches the tracker or the working tree. Without a remote, fall back to a local ref and say so. Make `go-getter plan status` compute a unit's state from the ledger and merged state, not from a marker alone. Add the pack answers in `src/packs/orchestration/` (`docs/skills/adding-a-practice-pack.md`), reword the generated guardrail `max-concurrent-agents-rule` to say the limit is per worker (decision 0169 item 2), and regenerate with `go-getter apply`.
Done-when: tests with a temporary bare remote show two concurrent claimants (separate processes, clocks offset by up to the margin) with exactly one succeeding, including overlapping scopes and a delegated child beside its parent, the loser retrying and being queued, no push that is not a fast-forward, a renewal writing only the heartbeat ref (ledger history unchanged), only under half the lease and never offline, an expired lease (plus the margin) taken over, a worker whose clock is beyond the margin refusing to claim, a parent with live children never expiring and an idle parent flagged, a parked claim never expiring, a release freeing the unit, an entry not authored by its holder or issuer ignored, a changed grant or scope ignored, a secret in a brief refused, renewal creating no tracker call and no working-tree change, and a missing remote falling back and saying so; `npm run check:generated` passes.
Result 2026-11-04 · `feat/k2-claims` · done: `compiler/src/work/scope.mjs` (scope overlap), `claim.mjs` (entry, validity, liveness, conflict rules, pure), `ledger.mjs` (ledger, heartbeat and checkpoint refs; fast-forward pushes only) and `ops.mjs` back `go-getter work claim|heartbeat|release`; every verb renews a claim under half its lease (`bin/go-getter.mjs`), which also covers the generated hooks since they call `go-getter check`. `plan status` lays live claims over the markers. Pack `orchestration@0.1.3` adds `lease-agent`, `lease-ci`, `lease-human`, `clock-skew`, `parent-idle` and `brief-max` (decisions 0189-0194, `draft` until the owner accepts them) and rewords `max-concurrent-agents-rule` to per worker. The guardrails stay `draft` until K16 (activation rule). Review fixes: a renewal writes only the worker's heartbeat ref, one commit naming the units held from the current worktree, and only that worktree renews them; a heartbeat dated beyond now plus the margin is ignored; readers reject an entry whose lease exceeds the configured one, whose expiry exceeds creation plus the lease or whose `created` is in the future, accept a takeover only after the holder's last heartbeat before it plus the lease plus the margin, and ignore a hand-written `parked` state; a ledger that is deleted or rewritten is `diverged` with the recovery `--accept-remote-ledger`; `--parent` is validated; the host clock is learned from the Date header (https remote, or `https://<host>/` for ssh and git remotes; only an absolute path or `file://` remote counts as this machine) and a claim is refused with `clock-unverified` when it cannot be learned (the owner may pass `--unverified-clock` for a unit nobody holds, with a loud warning; a takeover is never allowed then); a heartbeat made after the lease end plus the margin is ignored and a heartbeat refuses a lapsed lease (`lapsed`: claim again), so only a ledger write revives a claim; after its push a claim re-reads the ledger and reports `lost-race` unless readers see its entry as the live one; worker ids are `w-` plus a hash, stored once per worktree, and `..` and `.lock` ids are refused; the test clock needs `GO_GETTER_TEST_MODE=1`.
